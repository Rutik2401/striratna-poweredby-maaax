The Beginning

It started with 1,000+ unread messages

What began as a small Instagram page sharing beautiful jewellery designs grew faster than we ever imagined. Within months, we were drowning in messages every single day — customers asking prices, designs, delivery, how to order.

Our small team was overwhelmed. We were losing orders because we couldn't reply fast enough. So we decided to build something better — a proper home for स्त्रीरत्न.

"Amruta, have you read the comments? There are 1,000+ messages pending. We're packing orders but can't reply. Please understand, we will reply to everyone."

— Actual conversation from our team
